Metis

Metis is a private, personal AI assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

With the owner's permission it can read the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar and prepare email drafts. It cannot send, delete or change email, and it cannot change the calendar.

Nothing is offered to the public, nobody else can sign in, and no data is collected from visitors to this page.
